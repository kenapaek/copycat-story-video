# The 9-Beat Engine (mandatory arc)

| # | Beat | Time | Dur | Purpose | Camera |
|---|------|------|-----|---------|--------|
| 1 | Hook | 0–6 | 6s | Side-by-side sync gag + title. Establish the pair. | static medium |
| 2 | Montage A | 6–14 | 8s | Contrast gag #1 + HUD stat #1. Funny/relatable. | static medium |
| 3 | Montage B | 14–20 | 6s | Contrast gag #2, movement (walk/run). | static medium |
| 4 | Separation | 20–27 | 7s | The pair is split (leave / time passes). Sad close-up. | push-in |
| 5 | Twist | 27–40 | 13s | Reveal: the other one kept the bond alive (call/video/kept spot). HUD #2. | push-in |
| 6 | Softening | 40–44 | 4s | POV character melts. "kau rindu..." | close push-in |
| 7 | Reunion | 44–51 | 7s | Full-speed run + hug. Emotional peak. | over-shoulder |
| 8 | Reversal | 51–61 | 10s | MIRROR shot 1 (same set+action) — now POV copies the other. 2 captions. | static medium |
| 9 | End card | 61–65 | 4s | Title + real citation + share CTA + question. | blurred freeze |

## Adapting to any topic
- The two characters can be: two people, OR one person at two ages, OR person + pet.
- "Separation" can be literal (camp/work) or time passing (growing up).
- "Reunion" can be literal (hug) or metaphorical (rediscovering your inner kid).
- The REVERSAL is the soul of the format: whoever was copied/annoying becomes the one copied.
- HUD gags: 2 max, small caps, e.g. UMUR 6 / GEMBIRA 100% · TAHUN 2006 / KAU 6.

## Gold-standard example (approved): "Why do we became adults?"
1 hook: brush teeth sync, adult tired / kid excited — "dulu, kau pun macam dia."
2 kitchen: kid pours cereal melimpah / adult scroll phone — "dia gembira dengan benda kecil."
3 hallway: kid runs / adult walks slow — "dia berlari. kau dah lupa caranya."
4 door: kid waves, fades to photo — "satu hari, kau tinggalkan dia."
5 room_night: mak hantar video lama, kid laughing in rain — "rupanya dia masih ada dalam diri kau."
6 softening: adult smiles at phone — "kau rindu budak tu."
7 outside_rain: kid runs full speed → hug — "dia berlari ke arah kau."
8 bathroom mirror: both excited, foam flying — "kali ni, kau yang tiru dia." / "sebab dia tak pernah hilang."
9 end card: nostalgia citation + "HANTAR INI KEPADA KAWAN KAU" + "bila kali terakhir kau ketawa macam budak-budak?"
