# Caption Voice — Bahasa Melayu santai (default)

## Rules
- Second person: "kau". Warm, nostalgic, gently funny. Never cynical.
- Lowercase always. Short: ≤ 7 words, ≤ ~40 characters per caption.
- Malay-first. English only for words everyone uses (video, telefon OK as "telefon").
- No chat-speak abbreviations (jangan "x", "2", "ko"). Tulis penuh: "tak", "dah", "pun" OK.
- One caption per beat; beat 8 may have TWO (caption + caption2).
- Title: keep the user's exact wording (even English), 1 gold highlight word.

## Approved caption set — "Why do we became adults?"
1. dulu, kau pun macam dia.
2. dia gembira dengan benda kecil.
3. dia berlari. kau dah lupa caranya.
4. satu hari, kau tinggalkan dia.
5. rupanya dia masih ada dalam diri kau.
6. kau rindu budak tu.
7. dia berlari ke arah kau.
8. kali ni, kau yang tiru dia.  →  caption2: sebab dia tak pernah hilang.

## End card voice
- Line (small caps): plain true statement + REAL citation if confident.
  Contoh: "MENGIMBAS KENANGAN LAMA MEMBERI MAKNA KEPADA HIDUP (SEDIKIDES & WILDSCHUT, 2018)"
- CTA (gold, small caps): "HANTAR INI KEPADA KAWAN KAU"
- Question (gold): "bila kali terakhir kau ketawa macam budak-budak?"

## Citation rule (HARD)
Only cite studies you are confident exist (e.g. Sedikides & Wildschut 2018, nostalgia &
meaning; Howe et al. 2018, sibling imitation). If unsure → drop the citation entirely and
keep a plain true statement. NEVER invent authors, years, or findings.

## Manglish mode (if user asks)
Malay backbone + English verbs/nouns: "dia copy semua benda kau buat.", "kali ni, kau yang copy dia."
## English mode
Mirror the reference: "he copied every single thing.", "this time you copied him."
