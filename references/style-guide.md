# Style Guide — the visual DNA (locked)

## Format
- 1080×1920 (9:16), 30fps, 60–70s total. No watermark, no handle, no outro logo.

## Palette (hex)
- Wall peach #E9C9A8 · wall cream #F4E9D8 · trim white #F7F3EA
- Mustard #E8B33C (hero shirt) · mustard dark #C99427
- Teal #3E8E8C (bags) · terracotta #C4502E (curtain/lamp) · sage #7BA05B
- Grass #8FBF6F · wood floor #B5793F · wood dark #8A5A2B
- Skin #F2C49B · hair #4A2E1E · tee cream #F4F1E8 · stripe blue #A8CBE0
- Purple #9B7FC4 · camp green #3F7D3A · rain sky #9FB4A8
- Text white #FFFFFF · highlight gold #E8B33C
Materials: flat/matte (MeshLambert), soft warm ambient + one warm key light. No harsh shadows.

## Characters (low-poly)
- Rounded box head, helmet-style hair mesh, NO nose.
- Eyes: two dark rounded dots. Brows: thick short bars — ALL emotion = brow angle + mouth.
- Body: tapered box torso, simple tube limbs, mitten hands. Kid = same rig scaled ~0.62.
- Emotion table (brow angle / mouth):
  neutral 0° line · happy +8° smile · excited +12° open · annoyed −18° flat
  tired −6° half-lid eyes · sad +14° inner-up frown · soft +6° small smile

## Camera
- FOV ~35, eye level (y≈1.3–1.5), distance 3.6–4.5, static medium shots.
- Emotional beats: slow push-in 4–8% over the shot. Hug = over-shoulder close shot.
- Cuts only (hard cut), except separation may use fade.

## Sets (engine builds these)
- bathroom: oval sink counter, striped green curtain, shelf with plant + cups, mirror framing
- kitchen: window w/ green outside, table, cereal box "SUNNY O'S", bowls, faucet
- hallway: runner rug w/ diamond pattern, photo frames on walls, side table + lamp
- door: green front door, porch, fence, potted plant, grass + tree outside
- room_night: dim warm room, bed/desk, phone glow on face
- outside_rain: grey-green sky, rain particles, puddle, fence, tree

## Typography
- Title: serif (EB Garamond, fallback Georgia), top ~12% height, white, ONE word gold.
- Captions: italic serif, lowercase, white + soft shadow, bottom ~26% height, centered.
- HUD: small caps, letterspaced, left side ~45% height, 2 short lines.
- End card: blurred freeze of last frame + centered serif text block.

## Motion & gags
- Sync gag: both characters do the SAME action, kid offset 0–0.2s; head-tilt together.
- Particles: toothpaste foam (white puffs), cereal spill, rain streaks.
- Loop rule: shot 1 and shot 8 = same set + same action, emotions flipped.

## Audio
- Soft ambient pentatonic pluck bed (procedural, quiet). No voiceover.
- User adds trending sound later in CapCut/TikTok.
